This release adds a dedicated multiple sequence alignment workspace and makes primer melting-temperature analysis more accurate, consistent, and understandable.

## New Features

- **Multiple sequence alignment workspace** — open aligned FASTA projects with MUSCLE, navigate continuous residue tracks, inspect row and column highlights, and choose save or export options
- **MSA viewing improvements** — black residue colors, stable column-centered glyphs, reduced scroll rerendering, and a toolbar save-options menu

## Bug Fixes

- Corrected primer Tm for reverse alignments, mismatches, terminal mismatches, and dangling ends; unsupported gapped structures now explicitly fall back to the 3′ matching region
- Applied the current Tm conditions consistently across primer design, alignment details, and the primer overview, and preserved those settings between launches
- Clarified primer Tm conventions with concise `Full duplex` and `3′ matching region` labels and documented SnapGene/supplier-compatible defaults
- Improved MSA and FASTA dialog layout, sidebar spacing, continuous scrolling, and alignment navigator positioning
